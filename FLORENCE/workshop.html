About Our Workshops

Uganda Allied Farmers Association organizes workshops aimed at empowering farmers with practical skills and knowledge to improve productivity, sustainability, and profitability. Our workshops focus on modern agricultural practices, market linkages, and technology adoption.

Upcoming Workshops

  • Organic Farming Techniques: Learn how to grow crops using eco-friendly methods and fertilizers.
  • Post-Harvest Handling & Storage: Techniques to reduce crop losses and improve product quality.
  • Integrated Pest Management (IPM): Sustainable pest control methods for higher yields.
  • Agro-Processing and Value Addition: Transforming raw produce into market-ready products.
  • Farmers’ Market Strategies: How to identify buyers, negotiate prices, and access local and export markets.

Benefits of Attending

  • Gain practical, hands-on experience from experts.
  • Learn modern techniques to increase farm productivity.
  • Network with other farmers and potential buyers.
  • Access to free training materials and guides.
  • Certification that can enhance market trust for your products.

How to Participate

Interested farmers can register for workshops online or at our local offices. Early registration is encouraged as seats are limited. All workshops are conducted in both English and local languages to ensure clear understanding.

Past Workshop Highlights

Our past workshops have focused on coffee farming, sustainable maize production, dairy management, and agro-processing. Farmers who attended reported improved yields, better post-harvest handling, and stronger market access.
